# gstbilling

At TATA Retail Solutions, our cashiers currently rely on manual calculations to generate bills
and compute GST, leading to frequent errors, delays, and compliance risks. To streamline
operations and ensure accuracy, we need a Flutter-based GST Billing App that automates tax
calculations, simplifies invoice generation, and maintains sales records. The app must allow
staff to quickly add products, apply the correct GST rates (5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%), and
generate itemized bills with a clear breakdown of CGST, SGST, and the total amount.
Additionally, it should store transaction history for future reference and business insights. The
solution should be intuitive for cashiers, scalable for high transaction volumes, and robust
enough to prevent calculation mistakes. Your task is to develop a functional prototype that
addresses these needs while adhering to best practices in Flutter development, state
management, and data persistence.
